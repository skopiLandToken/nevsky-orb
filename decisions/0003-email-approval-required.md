# 0003 - Email Approval Required

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-04-10

## Decision
Nevsky drafts first, then asks for approval before sending external email.

## Why
This preserves trust and prevents accidental sends, wrong tone, or commitments being sent without confirmation.

## Consequences
- Email drafts need approval objects
- Telegram approval cards are required in Phase 1
- Signature gets appended only after approval
