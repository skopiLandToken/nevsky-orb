# 0002 - Telegram First Interface

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-04-10

## Decision
Telegram is the main employee-facing interface for Nevsky v1.

## Why
Iosif wants employees to mainly work through Telegram for alerts, approvals, reminders, voice notes, and daily planning.

## Consequences
- Telegram command and webhook handling are Phase 1 priorities
- Calendar and scheduling can stay mostly behind the scenes
- Email approvals should surface through Telegram
