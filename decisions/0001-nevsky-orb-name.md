# 0001 - Nevsky ORB Naming

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-04-10

## Decision
Use ORB as the main category term for Nevsky.

## Meaning
ORB = Operational Relationship Brain

## Why
CRM feels too narrow and outdated for the system being built.
Nevsky is meant to handle communication, planning, reminders, approvals, memory, and operational coordination.

## Notes
Iosif preferred a stronger category term that feels more alive and modern.

## Consequences
- Brand language should use Nevsky ORB
- Docs should treat ORB as the category term
- Future product copy should avoid describing Nevsky as only a CRM
