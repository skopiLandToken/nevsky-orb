II.What SKOpi actually is (in plain English)

The simplest way to say it: SKOpi is a company building tools that let regular people do what only big real estate firms and Wall Street insiders used to be able to do.

For a long time, if you wanted to put money into a real estate development — a piece of raw land, a subdivision, a build-out — you either had to be rich enough to buy the whole thing yourself, or you had to know somebody who'd let you into a private deal. The system was rigged so the same fifty firms saw every opportunity first, and everyone else got the leftovers years later when the real money was already made.

We're flipping that. The Reindeer property is the first deal we're using these tools on, and you're already in it with me. But Reindeer is one piece. SKOpi is the whole platform underneath.

There are a few moving parts and I want to walk you through each one plainly:

A way to raise money for projects without going to banks. This involves something called crypto tokens. Don't let that word scare you off — think of tokens like shares of stock, except instead of being only available to people with brokerage accounts and lawyers, anyone with a phone can buy them. Same idea as stock — you put money in, you own a piece of the project, you share in the profits when it works. Just without the middlemen taking their cut on every transaction.

A platform for understanding land at a level nobody else has. That's TERRA. I'll spend the next section on this because it matters more than people realize.

Our own document tools, our own email, our own communications. We don't run on Google or Microsoft. Everything we do sits on hardware we control. There's a reason for this — I'll get to it.

A real company structure underneath all of it. Delaware C-corp, board of directors, governance documents, the whole thing. This isn't a kitchen-table operation pretending to be a company. It's a company.

Every layer is owned by us instead of rented from somebody else. That's the difference between SKOpi and most companies that try to do something like this.

III.Why I built our own land intelligence layer (this is TERRA)

Here's the part most people don't get, and the part I want you to understand most.

When somebody wants to look at a property — figure out what it's worth, what it can be developed into, who owns it, what's wrong with it — they pull it up on Zillow or Redfin. Maybe their agent has access to the MLS. Those tools tell you the basics: square footage, last sale price, sometimes a guess at the value.

That's surface information. For real land deals, surface isn't enough.

When you're actually deciding whether to buy a piece of land — like the Reindeer property — you need to know: who's owned it for the last fifty years and at what prices, what permits have ever been pulled on it, whether there are easements running through it that nobody talks about, what the county valuation has been doing year over year, whether there are utility lines crossing it, what the zoning actually allows you to build, and a dozen other things that take a research analyst days to dig up one parcel at a time.

The big private equity land funds — the firms with billions of dollars — they have whole internal teams that do nothing but this kind of digging. They built their own systems. Small companies just don't have that. Small companies make do with Zillow and a lot of phone calls.

We built ours.

I want you to understand what I just said. We built our own land intelligence platform. Me, working on this, with the AI tools we've put together. Right now today, on my phone, I can drop a pin anywhere in Deschutes County, Oregon — anywhere, on any piece of land — and in seconds I get back the parcel boundaries, the owner of record, the full permit history, every sale that's ever been recorded on that parcel and at what price, the year-over-year valuation trend from the county, and any development documents on file. One hundred and six thousand parcels indexed. Every piece of land in the entire county, ready to query in under a second.

I'll give you a real example. The Reindeer property — the one you and I are buying — when I ran it through TERRA, the system pulled up the full sales chain on it. It caught that the property transferred from Lennar (the big national homebuilder) to Knox Investments (the LLC we're now buying it from) in December of 2024. That kind of information takes a research analyst hours to dig up. We get it in a tap.

Next week, the deeds and historical easement layer comes online — every recorded document on Deschutes parcels going back to 1871. After that, the same system extends to every county we want. Crook County, Jefferson County, Klamath County, eventually the whole state and beyond.

Here's what that means: by the time most other small real estate companies even realize they need this kind of platform, we'll be eighteen to twenty-four months ahead of them. The work we're doing right now is work nobody else is doing.

When I get to a property, Wade, I see things on my phone in thirty seconds that other land buyers spend a week of phone calls trying to figure out. That's the difference. That's TERRA.

IV.Why I'm not building this on top of someone else's stack

I want to talk to you about something you'll appreciate.

The way most companies work today — even most of the companies that look successful from the outside — is they're paying rent to somebody else. They use Google for email, Microsoft for documents, Amazon for servers, Stripe for payments, Mailchimp for newsletters, Zillow for property data. Every single one of those is a bill they pay every month, and every single one of those companies can shut them off, throttle them, change the terms, raise the price, or quietly hand their data to a competitor. It happens all the time.

I refuse to build SKOpi that way.

Our servers are ours. Our AI runs on hardware we control. Our document tools — we use a system called SVOIcloud, which is our own setup, sitting on our own equipment, that does what Google Docs does but without Google watching every word we write. Our land intelligence layer is ours. Our communications stack is ours.

It costs more upfront. It's harder to set up. It's slower at the start. And it pays back forever, because nobody owns the rails underneath us. Nobody can shut SKOpi off because nobody else holds the keys.

This is also part of why we use crypto tokens for raising money instead of going to banks. Banks have rules about who they'll lend to and what they'll lend to. They take a cut on every transaction. They can decline you, freeze your account, force you through their system. Tokens let SKOpi raise capital from regular people who want in, without asking permission from a bank or Wall Street. The people who put money in own a piece of the deal directly. No middleman, no permission slip.

That's not speculation. That's how rich people have invested in real estate forever. We're just opening that door to everyone else.
